System Architecture & NTRO PS-17 Compliance

National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO) · Problem Statement 17 Specifications

Problem Statement 17 Objective

To develop an advanced algorithmic software system capable of taking continuous single-pass drone video (1080p/4K) together with asynchronous GPS/GNSS and flight metadata, and generating a georeferenced, accurate 3D model with spatial error ≤ 1.0 m in under 15 minutes of compute time.

Technological Innovations

• Dynamic Object Masking: Integrates real-time YOLOv8 and Segment Anything Model (SAM) to isolate and mask moving vehicles, pedestrians, and dynamic vegetation before feature matching.
• Sim(3) 7-Parameter Georeferencing: Optimizes 3D rotation, translation, and absolute metric scale against GNSS/RTK fixes without requiring pre-surveyed Ground Control Points.
• Evidence-Aware Representation: Categorizes every vertex and triangle into Observed (direct line-of-sight), Reconstructed (multi-view stereo), Inferred (geometric priors), or Unknown (occluded voids).
