Pipeline Architecture

How AeroSculpt Builds 3D Models

AeroSculpt transforms overlapping drone video into realistic, true-scale 3D models in four simple steps—similar to how modern 3D mapping and Google Earth create digital twins.

1

1. Capture & Filter

The drone records the site from multiple angles. Blurry or shaky frames are automatically filtered out, keeping only crystal-clear images.

2

2. Track & Match

Common landmarks and visual points are matched across frames to calculate the exact position and angle of the camera for each shot.

3

3. Build 3D Shape

By connecting matching points from different viewpoints, a dense 3D point cloud and solid mesh surface of the terrain and buildings are formed.

4

4. Texture & Render

High-resolution photographic colors are projected onto the 3D model, generating a lightweight, realistic digital twin ready for web inspection.

PRECISION

Centimeter-Level Accuracy (±1.2 cm)

Achieves survey-grade spatial accuracy certified against physical Ground Control Points across the entire survey perimeter.

  • Mean reprojection error of only 0.42 px across all registered camera stations.
  • Millimeter-level residual error after 7-DoF Sim(3) Helmert transformation.
  • Ensures true-to-life 1:1 metric scale for volume, area, and distance analysis.
DATUM

Standard Global CRS (EPSG:32630)

Reconstructed models and orthophotos are directly projected into standard cartographic coordinate systems.

  • Projected directly to WGS 84 / UTM Zone 30N (EPSG:32630).
  • Seamlessly imports into industry GIS tools (QGIS, ArcGIS, AutoCAD, Civil 3D).
  • Exports georeferenced GeoTIFF orthomosaics and Digital Surface Models (DSM).
CALIBRATION

Automated Sensor Self-Calibration

Self-calibrates camera optics during flight to remove wide-angle lens distortion automatically.

  • Resolves principal point offsets and focal length parameters dynamically.
  • Compensates for radial (k1, k2) and tangential (p1, p2) lens distortion.
  • Guarantees distortion-free perspective geometry across every captured frame.
