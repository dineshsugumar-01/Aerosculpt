NTRO Problem Statement 17 Documentation

Single-pass UAV photogrammetry, real-time georeferencing and evidence-aware 3D reconstruction.

1. Operational Overview

AeroSculpt addresses the core challenge of NTRO Problem Statement 17 (SH/26158): converting raw, single-pass continuous aerial video collected by UAVs into accurate, georeferenced, metric 3D digital surfaces and point clouds without requiring multi-pass crisscross flights.

2. Sensor Synchronization & Trajectory Verification

The system synchronizes video frames with asynchronous 10 Hz GNSS records and 100 Hz IMU telemetry. The flight path validation verifies that spatial coverage satisfies stereoscopic parallax requirements (overlap > 65%) while rejecting high-blur or turn-induced degraded keyframes.

3. Evidence-Aware 3D Representation

Unlike conventional black-box photogrammetry, AeroSculpt categorizes every triangle and vertex into 4 strict confidence tiers: Observed (Direct Optical Multi-view rays), Reconstructed (Stereo Match), Inferred (Occluded / Interpolated), and Unknown (Unsurveyed Blindspots).

4. Verified Standards Compliance

Meets ASPRS Positional Accuracy Standards for Digital Geospatial Data (Class 1, RMSE < 0.05m with RTK fixed telemetry). Output formats include textured GLB/OBJ meshes, georeferenced LAS/PLY point clouds, and orthorectified GeoTIFF rasters.
